01 / The opportunity

Less administration. More actual coaching.

Training plans, videos, exercise instructions and body measurements often live in separate messages and spreadsheets. Fat2Fit puts the complete invited coach–student loop into a familiar Telegram Mini App.

One product for the planning, the session, the review and the evidence of progress.

01 / PLAN

Coach workspace

Invite students, create exercises and templates or assemble an ad-hoc workout in seconds.

02 / TRAIN

Real execution

Track sets, reps and weights; let a coach assist in a live session without losing attribution.

03 / REVIEW

Feedback loops

Review completed workouts and private technique videos with actionable comments.

04 / MEASURE

Visible progress

Collect dated body metrics and compare the first and most recent results.

05 / FOCUS

Coach action center

See today's schedule, tomorrow's workload and items awaiting review together.

06 / RESPECT PRIVACY

Independent relationships

Each coach manages their own students; shared students keep coach-specific workout histories.

02 / Product decisions

The details that made the MVP useful.

The first version was not a feature catalog. It was a working coaching flow. Real device feedback shaped subsequent versions, including mobile fixes, coach self-training and faster workout creation.

Explore the decisions in depth ↗
Telegram-first

Meet users where they already coordinate; accept WebView and platform limitations.

Templates + ad-hoc workouts

Support repeatable programs without making every spontaneous session into a template.

Completion ≠ approval

A finished workout is not necessarily reviewed. Separate states help coaches prioritize.

Progress without fake judgments

Show before, after and delta. Don't automatically claim weight gain or loss is “good”.

Independent coach ownership

One student can work with several coaches, without inadvertently pooling their private data.

03 / From concept to pilot

A complete product loop, not just a mockup.

01Define the core job: invite → plan → execute → review → measure.
02Build the minimum viable architecture: verified Telegram identity, server-side authorization and PostgreSQL persistence.
03Ship a private live pilot: Next.js on Vercel, database and storage in Supabase.
04Iterate on feedback: ad-hoc training, coach self-use, permissions, iOS layout, video and workout review, progress reporting.
05Measure honestly: working product and actual usage feedback, but no invented revenue, growth or retention metrics.
04 / Architecture and delivery

Built for a real app,
not a throwaway prototype.

Verified Telegram authentication, HttpOnly sessions, server-enforced coach–student permissions, private video storage, dated reports, automated validation and production delivery.
